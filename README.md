## my favorite series

** Alien Stage **

It is a short webcomic + web series on youtube by *VIVINOS* and *QMENG*

The story revolves around a dystopian future where aliens dominated the world and treated the remaining surviving humans as pets. To entertain themselves, the aliens hosted a massive, high stakes singing competition called the "Alien Stage" where the stories of six characters. Instead of talking scenes and dialogues, the series features different music videos and tracks that drive the story.
